How each tick's backward sweep ended

Stepping the original mutant backward in 0.1s increments, each tick's sweep stops the first time the replay no longer reproduces the recorded failure ("diverged"), the ego leaves the simulation, or there's no earlier recorded tick left to step back to ("reached scenario start" — the failure reproduces at every available lead time).
