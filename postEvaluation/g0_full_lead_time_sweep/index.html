Is reproduction monotonic in lead time?

For each tick, scanning every swept lead time in increasing order: does the recorded failure ever reproduce again after already failing to reproduce at a nearer lead time? A state-based autopilot/mutant (or state-based background traffic) has no obligation to decay smoothly, so this isn't assumed — it's checked directly.
