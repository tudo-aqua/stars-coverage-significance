Is reproduction monotonic in lead time?

For each tick, scanning every swept lead time in increasing order: does the recorded failure ever reproduce again after already failing to reproduce at a nearer lead time? A state-based autopilot/mutant (or state-based background traffic) has no obligation to decay smoothly, so this isn't assumed — it's checked directly. (The sweep's final, full-scenario-replay step is excluded from this check — see the "Full-replay mismatches" note below.)
