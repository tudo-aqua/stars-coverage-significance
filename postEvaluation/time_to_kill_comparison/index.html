Time To Kill Explorer

Reads CSVs directly from baseline_next_tick/time_to_kill/ and draw_ticks_with_decision_tree_grouping/time_to_kill/, plus decision_tree_comparison/decision_tree_comparison.json for the expected-value comparison — no build/generation step. Expected vs. actual

E(k/N), E(equal) and E(weight) come from decision_tree_comparison.json's significance card (see that page's Statistics tab for the definitions) for the selected decision tree run. Actual mean is this page's own empirically observed mean draws-to-kill for the strategy that most closely matches each estimator's sampling policy: E(k/N) ↔ uniform random, E(equal) ↔ unweighted leaf round-robin, E(weight) ↔ significance-weighted leaf sampling (only available for the tick-level dataset). Actual ÷ Expected below 1 (green) means the mutant was killed faster in practice than the estimator predicted; above 1 (red, shown once it exceeds 2×) means slower.
