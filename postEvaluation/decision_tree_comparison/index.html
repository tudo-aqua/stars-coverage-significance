Leaf sizes & failure probability

    
Per-leaf total tick count, failing tick count, and pl — the probability that a tick drawn from that leaf kills at least one mutant (failing ticks ÷ total ticks). wl and pl·wl are the significance weight used by the “Expected ticks to next failure” card below: wl = pl ÷ Σpl (this leaf's share of the summed failure probability across every leaf of the run, regardless of the checkbox above). Both move in lockstep with pl, so sorting by pl also orders them. Click a column header to sort.

Expected ticks to next failure

    

      Three estimators for how many ticks you'd need to sample before hitting a failure (or a specific mutant kill), built from every leaf of this run — independent of the “only leaves that kill” checkbox above, since a weighting scheme has to account for the whole tree, not just the leaves currently listed. Every column feeding the three estimators is included below, so each row can be recomputed independently. E(k/N) = N ÷ k: uniform random sampling over the entire database (N = total ticks ever recorded); for “Any monitor failure” k is the database-wide failing-tick count, per mutant it's this run's own count of ticks that killed that mutant (same number as the “Ticks (this run)” column). E(equal) = L ÷ Σp: this run's L leaves sampled with equal probability, where L is the tree's tuned leaf count (“Best num_leaves” on the Overview tab) — not just the leaves listed in the tables above, since a leaf that never received a single evaluation tick still counts toward L but has no row of its own. E(weight) = 1 ÷ Σ(p·w): leaves sampled in proportion to their overall significance wl (defined in the leaf table above, from the “Any monitor failure” probabilities), asking how that one proposed sampling policy would perform at finding each individual mutant — Σ(p·w) is exactly the row total (Σ) column of the “Weighted probability” table below, and this estimator is unaffected by L. Lower is better in every E(…) column — it means fewer ticks needed on average.

Probability of killing each mutant, by leaf (plm)

    
Rows are mutants, columns are leaves. Cell (m, l) is plm — the probability that a tick drawn from leaf l kills mutant m (killing ticks for m in l ÷ total ticks in l). The header also carries each leaf's total tick count, total killing-tick count (summed across mutants), and its wl significance weight, for reference. Multiplying a row's plm cells by the wl header row and summing gives Σ(plm·wl) for that mutant — the value the “Expected ticks to next failure” card's E(weight) column inverts, useful for cross-checking it by hand. Click a leaf column (or the corner) to sort the mutant rows by it.

    

      
      
      
    

    

      

        
        
      

    

  


  

    
Weighted probability of killing each mutant, by leaf (plm·wl)

    
Rows are mutants, columns are leaves. Cell (m, l) is plm·wl — leaf l's plm for mutant m multiplied by leaf l's own significance weight wl (defined in the leaf table above, from the “Any monitor failure” probabilities — the same wl for every mutant, not recomputed per mutant). This is the exact per-leaf term the “Expected ticks to next failure” card's E(weight) sums: the row total (Σ) column is Σ(plm·wl), and that mutant's E(weight) is 1 ÷ Σ. Use this table to cross-check E(weight) cell by cell against an external reference. The header also carries each leaf's total tick count, total killing-tick count, and wl, for reference. Click a leaf column (or the corner) to sort the mutant rows by it.
