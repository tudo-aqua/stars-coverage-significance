Run details
How these numbers are worked out
Time to kill
One plus the position, in the strategy's order, of the first drawn starting scenario that kills the mutant. Every order covers the whole pool of valid scenarios, so every scoring mutant is killed eventually.
Training and scoring mutants
The trees, greedy and the weighted strategy learn from the training mutants; all times are measured on the scoring mutants. In the full run both are the same mutants, so greedy is an upper bound there. With rare_below_…, training uses the mutants killed in at least that share of the pool and scoring the rarer ones.
Speedup vs random
For each scoring mutant, random drawing's median time to kill divided by the strategy's median, then the geometric mean over the mutants. "Faster than random" counts the mutants whose median is lower than random's.
Draws to kill all
Per repetition, the largest time to kill over the scoring mutants; shown as the median and the 25th to 75th percentile over the repetitions.
Random check
Written by the draw step: the largest |z| of random drawing's mean time to kill against the exact expectation (N + 1) / (K + 1). Below 4 is expected.
