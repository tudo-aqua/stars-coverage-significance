Tick Vehicle Visualizer

Paste or upload a tick's vehicle-snapshot JSON (the shape of all_vehicles_json / TickVehicleSnapshot[]) to see every vehicle's lane and position.

Input

Fields expected per vehicle: id, ego (bool, optional), type, lane, front, back, speed, accel.

Scene

X: position along the lane (m), increasing to the right = direction of travel. Y: lane index, lane 0 at the bottom (rightmost, per SUMO convention). Scroll/pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Hover a vehicle for its full data.
